# Roadmap

Ideas for future work, in no particular order. Nothing here is committed; an item moves out when a real issue or branch takes it.

## Guitar support

- **Guitar tab support.** Read tablature staves from scores and show them where the piece calls for them, alongside or instead of the standard staff.
- **Guitar-Pro files.** Import `.gp3`–`.gp5`, `.gpx` and `.gp` files into the library, converted to the same Score the app builds from MusicXML.

## Play screen

- **Movable visual elements.** Let the user drag panels on the play screen — the Harmony display, and anything else that follows — so each can arrange their own visual space.
- **Out-of-scale keys.** A setting to enable or disable keys outside the current scale for learning, with those keys marked visually on the keyboard.
- **Arpeggios in the falling notes.** Carry arpeggio markings from the score into the falling notes, so the roll shows them where the notation does.
